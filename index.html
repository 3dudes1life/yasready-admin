Privacy

Privacy Policy

Free beta draft · August 16, 2026

What this beta stores

The current free beta does not require an account. Your business name, onboarding selections, and roadmap/checklist progress are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. That information is not synced to YasReady servers by this static beta.

Analytics

This build includes a privacy-conscious local event layer so the product can be wired to production analytics later. It does not send your roadmap answers or business name to an analytics service in this static release.

External websites

Business 101 may link to government agencies and other third-party services. Their privacy practices and terms apply when you leave YasReady.

Deleting your beta data

Open Settings and choose Start over. After you type RESET, YasReady removes the locally stored business and roadmap progress from that browser.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support contact shown on the Support page.
